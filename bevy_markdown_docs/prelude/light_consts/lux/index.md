# Module lux 

[Source](https://docs.rs/bevy_light/0.19.0/x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu/src/bevy_light/lib.rs.html#130)

Predefined for lux values in several locations.

The **lux** (symbol: **lx**) is the unit of [illuminance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illuminance), or [luminous flux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luminous_flux) per unit area, in the [International System of Units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_System_of_Units) (SI). It is equal to one lumen per square meter.

For more information, see [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lux)

## Constants

[AMBIENT\_DAYLIGHT](constant.AMBIENT_DAYLIGHT.html "constant bevy::prelude::light_consts::lux::AMBIENT_DAYLIGHT")

The amount of light (lux) from ambient daylight (not direct sunlight). This is the default for [`DirectionalLight`](../../struct.DirectionalLight.html "struct bevy::prelude::DirectionalLight")s in Bevy.

[CIVIL\_TWILIGHT](constant.CIVIL_TWILIGHT.html "constant bevy::prelude::light_consts::lux::CIVIL_TWILIGHT")

The amount of light (lux) during the dark limit of civil twilight under a clear sky.

[CLEAR\_SUNRISE](constant.CLEAR_SUNRISE.html "constant bevy::prelude::light_consts::lux::CLEAR_SUNRISE")

The amount of light (lux) during sunrise or sunset on a clear day.

[DARK\_OVERCAST\_DAY](constant.DARK_OVERCAST_DAY.html "constant bevy::prelude::light_consts::lux::DARK_OVERCAST_DAY")

The amount of light (lux) in very dark overcast day

[DIRECT\_SUNLIGHT](constant.DIRECT_SUNLIGHT.html "constant bevy::prelude::light_consts::lux::DIRECT_SUNLIGHT")

The amount of light (lux) in direct sunlight.

[FULL\_DAYLIGHT](constant.FULL_DAYLIGHT.html "constant bevy::prelude::light_consts::lux::FULL_DAYLIGHT")

The amount of light (lux) in full daylight (not direct sun).

[FULL\_MOON\_NIGHT](constant.FULL_MOON_NIGHT.html "constant bevy::prelude::light_consts::lux::FULL_MOON_NIGHT")

The amount of light (lux) during a full moon on a clear night.

[HALLWAY](constant.HALLWAY.html "constant bevy::prelude::light_consts::lux::HALLWAY")

The amount of light (lux) in an office building’s hallway/toilet lighting.

[LIVING\_ROOM](constant.LIVING_ROOM.html "constant bevy::prelude::light_consts::lux::LIVING_ROOM")

The amount of light (lux) in family living room lights.

[MOONLESS\_NIGHT](constant.MOONLESS_NIGHT.html "constant bevy::prelude::light_consts::lux::MOONLESS_NIGHT")

The amount of light (lux) in a moonless, overcast night sky. (starlight)

[OFFICE](constant.OFFICE.html "constant bevy::prelude::light_consts::lux::OFFICE")

The amount of light (lux) in an office.

[OVERCAST\_DAY](constant.OVERCAST_DAY.html "constant bevy::prelude::light_consts::lux::OVERCAST_DAY")

The amount of light (lux) on an overcast day; typical TV studio lighting

[RAW\_SUNLIGHT](constant.RAW_SUNLIGHT.html "constant bevy::prelude::light_consts::lux::RAW_SUNLIGHT")

The amount of light (lux) of raw sunlight, not filtered by the atmosphere.
